Exhibition Floor Strategy

Castor Oil Derivatives & Petrochemical Co-Formulation

Market Position: India commands ~85–90% of global castor seed production, yet true margin stability lies downstream in value-added derivatives rather than commercial crude oil. Even if the floor leans toward petrochemical giants, castor derivatives connect directly via functional substitution (bio-based vs. fossil feedstocks) and co-formulation (polyols, plasticizers, and cross-linkers).

Tier 1 • Volume / Base Standard Grades

First-Generation / Standard

Derivative Products:

Commercial Grade (BSS), Refined Castor Oil (FSG), Pale Pressed Grade (PPG)

Core End-Use Industries:

Paints, lubricants, basic pharma

Key Notes to Take:

Base crushing capacity, bulk delivery terms, price benchmarks per MT.

Tier 2 • Core Derivatives Flakes / Powders

Hydrogenated & Fatty Acids

Derivative Products:

Hydrogenated Castor Oil (HCO / Castor Wax), 12-Hydroxystearic Acid (12-HSA), Ricinoleic Acid

Core End-Use Industries:

Lithium multipurpose greases, cosmetics, rubber, hot-melt adhesives

Key Notes to Take:

Melting point specs, acid value, flake/powder mesh size, packaging stability.

Tier 3 • Pyrolysis / Cracking High-Spec Industrial

Specialty / Industrial

Derivative Products:

Dehydrated Castor Oil (DCO), Blown Oil, Sebacic Acid, Undecylenic Acid, Heptaldehyde

Core End-Use Industries:

Alkyd resins, high-performance coatings, aviation greases, nylon synthesis

Key Notes to Take:

Purity levels, color Gardner index, drying time, byproduct handling (e.g., 2-octanol yields).

Tier 4 • Advanced Downstream High Margin

High-Margin / Bio-based Polymers

Derivative Products:

Polyamide 11 (PA-11), Bio-polyols, Ester quats, Ethoxylated Castor Oil (PEG-EL series)

Core End-Use Industries:

Automotive light-weighting, 3D printing filaments, polyurethane foams, textile surfactants

Key Notes to Take:

Technical data sheets (TDS), regulatory compatibility (REACH, FDA, ISO).

Part 1 • Section 3

3. Downstream Buyers & Application Segments to Scout

Visit exhibitors representing industries that consume castor derivatives to understand buyer demand specs.

Lubricants & Grease Formulators

Castor wax (HCO) and 12-HSA are core thickening agents in lithium and lithium-complex greases. Ask what viscosity and consistency requirements they look for in suppliers.

Personal Care, Cosmetics & Pharma

High-purity PEG castor oil, cold-pressed castor oil, and esters are used in lipsticks, skin emulsions, and injectable drug delivery systems. Note their solvent residue limits and microbiological standards.

Paints, Coatings & Inks

Inquire about their demand for Dehydrated Castor Oil (DCO) fatty acids as non-yellowing alkyd drying agents.

Agrochemicals & Surfactants

Castor ethoxylates act as emulsifiers in crop protection formulations. Note the ethylene oxide (EO) mole ratios (e.g., 30, 36, 40, 54 moles) most in demand.

Part 2 • Zero Dedicated Castor Stalls? Pivot Strategy

Turning a Petrochemical-Heavy Expo into Your Advantage

Petrochemicals and castor oil derivatives are directly intertwined through 1) Functional Substitution (bio-based vs. fossil feedstocks) and 2) Co-Formulation (petrochemical resins requiring castor plasticizers, polyols, or cross-linkers). Use this playbook when surrounded by polymer and petrochemical booths.

Part 2 • Section 1

1. Re-frame Petrochemical Stalls as Your Target Customers & Competitors

Target these 4 specific petrochemical downstream booths and ask the exact transition questions below.

Polyols / PU Bio-Polyol Link

Polyurethane (PU) & Polyol Manufacturers

The Link: Conventional PU uses petrochemical polyols derived from propylene oxide/petroleum. Castor oil is the only natural, commercially abundant bio-polyol (containing natural hydroxyl groups).

What to Ask:

"Are your clients demanding bio-based polyurethane or ESG-compliant polyols for automotive seating/insulation foams?"

What to Note: Benchmark petrochemical polyol prices, hydroxyl values, and whether they have evaluated blending castor-based polyols.

Resins / Engineering Plastics PA-11 Link

Specialty Polymers & Nylon/Polyamide Compounds

The Link: Standard Polyamide 6 and 6,6 are pure petrochemicals. Polyamide 11 (PA-11) is 100% bio-based, derived directly from 11-aminoundecanoic acid (from castor).

What to Ask:

"Do you supply bio-polyamides or bio-based engineering plastics? What grade specs are your compounder clients asking for?"

What to Note: Grade specifications, compounder demand, heat/flexibility performance gaps vs. PA-6/6,6.

Lubricants & Base-Stocks HCO & 12-HSA Link

Synthetic Lubricant & Grease Base-Stock Producers

The Link: Synthetic esters and Group III/IV mineral oils compete with or formulate alongside Hydrogenated Castor Oil (Castor Wax) and 12-HSA thickeners.

What to Ask:

"Which thickeners and bio-lubricant base oils are you pairing with your synthetic esters for extreme-pressure (EP) industrial greases?"

What to Note: Thickener pairing specs, extreme-pressure formulation needs, bio-lubricant blending appetite.

Surfactants / EO Reactors Toll Ethoxylation Link

Surfactant & Ethoxylate Manufacturers

The Link: Many petrochemical ethoxylators react ethylene oxide (EO) with nonylphenol or fatty alcohols. The same reactors can make Ethoxylated Castor Oil (PEG-EL series) for agrochemical and cosmetic emulsifiers.

What to Ask:

"Do you do toll manufacturing/contract ethoxylation for natural oil fatty chains or vegetable oils like castor?"

What to Note: Spare EO reactor capacity, minimum batch size for job work, agro/cosmetic emulsifier capabilities.

Stall Conversation Cheat Sheet

Rapid-Fire Questions to Ask

For Castor Processors & Traders:
  • "Do you have your own crushing plant in Kutch/Banaskantha/Mehsana, or buy crude castor externally?"
  • "What is your trial batch MOQ vs. full container load (FCL) pricing?"
  • "Do you hold active EU REACH registration, US FDA/DMF, and Kosher/Halal certificates?"
  • "Can you supply in 1 MT IBC totes or 24 MT flexitanks with private/neutral labeling?"
For Petrochemical & Polymer Stalls (Pivot):
  • PU / Polyols: "Are your clients demanding bio-based polyurethane or ESG-compliant polyols for automotive seating/insulation foams?"
  • Nylon / Compounds: "Do you supply bio-polyamides (PA-11) or bio-based engineering plastics? What grade specs are compounders asking for?"
  • Synthetic Lubes: "Which thickeners (12-HSA / Castor Wax) and bio-lubricant base oils are you pairing with synthetic esters for EP greases?"
  • Surfactants: "Do you do toll manufacturing/contract ethoxylation for natural oil fatty chains or castor oil?"
For India ProMach Equipment Vendors:
  • "Have you installed batch hydrogenation loops or flaking units for 12-HSA or castor wax in Gujarat or Maharashtra recently?"

const SPREADSHEET_ID = '1G6DVMS9WX29V0VhMmij-GEedqmhrEVGWz_9KY_coJOY';

function doPost(e) {
  const ss = SpreadsheetApp.openById(SPREADSHEET_ID);
  const payload = JSON.parse(e.postData.contents);
  const items = Array.isArray(payload.records) ? payload.records : [payload];

  items.forEach(function(n) {
    if (n.type === 'castor') {
      const sheet = ss.getSheetByName('Castor_Stall_Notes') || ss.insertSheet('Castor_Stall_Notes');
      sheet.appendRow([
        n.id || '', n.timestamp || '', n.company || '', n.contact || '',
        n.booth || '', n.location || '', n.sourcing || '',
        n.prod1 || '', n.spec1 || '', n.prod2 || '', n.spec2 || '',
        (n.packaging || []).join('; '), n.moq || '', n.capacity || '',
        n.markets || '', (n.certs || []).join('; '), (n.actions || []).join('; '),
        n.sampleQty || '', n.extraNotes || ''
      ]);
    } else {
      const sheet = ss.getSheetByName('Petrochem_Pivot_Notes') || ss.insertSheet('Petrochem_Pivot_Notes');
      sheet.appendRow([
        n.id || '', n.timestamp || '', n.company || '', n.contact || '',
        (n.lines || []).join('; '), n.decarb || '', n.biocarbon || '',
        n.tollCap || '', n.batch || '', n.priceBench || '', n.perfGap || '',
        n.machVendor || '', n.machSetup || '', n.machCapex || ''
      ]);
    }
  });

  return ContentService.createTextOutput(JSON.stringify({ status: 'ok', count: items.length }))
    .setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.JSON);
}

function doGet() {
  const ss = SpreadsheetApp.openById(SPREADSHEET_ID);
  const out = [];
  const cSheet = ss.getSheetByName('Castor_Stall_Notes');
  if (cSheet && cSheet.getLastRow() > 1) {
    const rows = cSheet.getRange(2, 1, cSheet.getLastRow() - 1, 19).getValues();
    rows.forEach(function(r) {
      out.push({
        id: String(r[0]), timestamp: String(r[1]), type: 'castor',
        company: String(r[2]), contact: String(r[3]), booth: String(r[4]),
        location: String(r[5]), sourcing: String(r[6]), prod1: String(r[7]),
        spec1: String(r[8]), prod2: String(r[9]), spec2: String(r[10]),
        packaging: String(r[11]).split('; ').filter(Boolean),
        moq: String(r[12]), capacity: String(r[13]), markets: String(r[14]),
        certs: String(r[15]).split('; ').filter(Boolean),
        actions: String(r[16]).split('; ').filter(Boolean),
        sampleQty: String(r[17]), extraNotes: String(r[18]), synced: true
      });
    });
  }
  const pSheet = ss.getSheetByName('Petrochem_Pivot_Notes');
  if (pSheet && pSheet.getLastRow() > 1) {
    const rows = pSheet.getRange(2, 1, pSheet.getLastRow() - 1, 14).getValues();
    rows.forEach(function(r) {
      out.push({
        id: String(r[0]), timestamp: String(r[1]), type: 'petro',

  return ContentService.createTextOutput(JSON.stringify({ records: out }))
    .setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.JSON);
}
